# Third-party assets

- Lora, Outfit and Manrope fonts are bundled via the pinned `@fontsource` packages. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included alongside this document.
- GSAP 3.15.0 and its ScrollTrigger plugin are installed from the official `gsap` npm package and bundled locally. Their source preserves the package's copyright and license notices; see `https://gsap.com/standard-license/` for its licensing terms.
- Vite and Playwright are development/build tools installed through the package lockfile.
- The six client photographs and promotional images were supplied by the user and recovered unchanged from the approved standalone HTML. Their provenance is documented separately. No stock images were substituted.

- React and React DOM 19.3.0 (MIT), https://react.dev/. Used as progressively integrated components for the dual experience, scene controls and personal training enquiry. Bundled locally in production and the standalone file.
- Gym dumbbells, palms, sauna and steam are original SVG/CSS illustrations in this source; they are not photographs of the business's facilities.
